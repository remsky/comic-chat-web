Privacy Policy

What the rooms hold

Rooms are public and unencrypted at rest. Everyone connected to a room sees every message in it, and anyone who joins later receives the recent history. There is no private messaging and no end to end encryption. Your connection to the server is encrypted in transit over HTTPS and WSS.

When you send a message, the room stores:

Stored Why
Your nickname and the character you picked To draw and label the panel
The message text and balloon type To render the strip and replay recent history to new joiners
Pose, expression, and background choices To reproduce the same panel for everyone
The nicknames your message addressed To point your avatar at them when the panel is drawn again
A timestamp Recorded with the event and replayed with it to everyone in the room
A random identifier generated by your browser To tell one seat in the room from another, so the roster and your own avatar stay consistent across a reconnect

That random identifier is created in your browser, is not derived from anything about you or your device, and is not linked to any account, because there are none. Clearing your browser storage discards it and a new one is generated.

How long it is kept

Each room keeps a rolling window of its most recent 100 events. Older events are deleted automatically as new ones arrive. Nothing is archived or backed up by us beyond that window. Bear in mind that anyone in the room can screenshot or copy what they see, and we cannot undo that.

What we do not collect

No accounts, no email addresses, no passwords. We set no cookies, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We keep no request logs of our own: the platform can record the IP address behind each connection, and we have that recording turned off, so no copy of it reaches us. We do have it count requests per IP address to block floods, which happens on its systems and never reaches us. What our hosting provider keeps for its own purposes is described below, and we remain accountable for what it handles on our behalf.

Traffic measurement

This deployment runs Cloudflare Web Analytics, which loads a small script from Cloudflare on each page. It measures page views and load timings, and records the country, referring link, path, browser, operating system, and device type of a visit. The room you join is part of the URL, so the recorded path can name it. Your nickname, your messages, and the random identifier above are no part of this and are never sent to it. The script is not served to visitors in the European Union, so nothing is measured for them. This data is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What our hosting provider sees

The service runs on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. As the network and hosting provider, Cloudflare processes connection metadata including IP addresses in order to route traffic, protect against abuse, and apply rate limits, and it retains operational request logs. That processing is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy. Room data lives in Durable Object storage on Cloudflare's infrastructure, which spans multiple countries. Your messages may therefore be stored and processed in a country other than your own or the operator's, and be subject to the laws of the country holding them.

When we may disclose

We do not sell room data and there is no third party we hand it to. We may disclose what we hold where a law, court order, or lawful request from an authority requires it, where we are obliged to report unlawful content, or where we believe in good faith that it is necessary to prevent serious harm to someone. In practice the rolling window above means there is usually little left for us to disclose, and a request to identify a sender is one only our hosting provider can answer.

What your browser stores locally

These stay on your device and are not sent to us except where noted above:

  • your nickname and chosen character, only if you tick "Remember me";
  • the random identifier described above, which is sent with your messages;
  • display preferences such as the modern tweaks toggle, nametags, plain text view, and sidebar width.

Clearing site data in your browser removes all of it. Two short-lived entries in session storage carry your details between rooms when you switch, and the captured transcript to the report page, which deletes it as it loads. Session storage is discarded when the tab closes.

The Studio

The strip editor at /studio runs entirely in your browser. Strips are composed, rendered, and exported locally, and are never uploaded or stored by us. The one exception: while the word filter is enabled, balloon text is sent to the server to be checked against the filter and the result returned. That text is not logged or retained.

Your choices

Because the service has no accounts, we have no way to identify which messages are yours, so we cannot look them up or delete them on request. In practice they leave the room on their own once 100 newer events have arrived. If a specific message needs to come down sooner, tell us which room and roughly when, and we will remove what we can find.
